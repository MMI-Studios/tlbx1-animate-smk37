Animate device storage setup

This utility initializes sixteen on-device sound-patch slots on the Animate 982 firmware. It does not install firmware. It leaves the application, calibration and controller identity areas untouched.

Initialization erases the dedicated patch area. Audio pauses during writes; initialization can take several seconds. Keep the controller connected until the result appears. No controller is accessed until you connect. This workflow has been checked with simulated devices; physical validation remains outstanding.
