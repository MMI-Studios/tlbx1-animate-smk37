Enable audio, then play the keys. The sequencer starts empty.

Home: SHIFT + GLOBE returns home. Pads 1–4 open slots A–D, 5/6 open X/Y modulation, 7 opens mixer, 8 sequencer, 9 patches, 10 global, 11 masks, 12 routing, 13 effects, 15 Save and 16 visualizers. PARAM/MASK/MOD also use plain pads 1–4 for slots and 5/6 for X/Y.

Patches: PATCH opens the browser; arrows or K1 browse. PATCH again requests Load; SHIFT + PATCH requests Save. Pad 1 confirms and pad 2 cancels. Six factory patches and sixteen user slots hold sound settings; sequence steps stay in the current project. User patches here persist in this browser, separate from device flash. Export project keeps a portable file including the sequence.

Shortcuts: Hold BT / SHIFT and press pad 1 for lanes, 2 steps, 3 parameters, 4 mixer, 5 next slot envelope, 6 masks, 7 routing, 8 X modulation, or 16 visualizers. GLOBE opens global controls. Arrows change banks/pages.

Mixer knobs: K1 A volume · K2 A pan · K3 B volume · K4 B pan · K5 C volume · K6 C pan · K7 D volume · K8 D pan. PARAM faders edit the selected slot’s attack, decay, sustain and release.

Keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; plays notes. Space starts/stops. Mouse, touch and keyboard holds release on focus loss.
